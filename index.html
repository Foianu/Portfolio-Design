# Portfolio

+ My name is Valentina, born in 93', I work in Design Industry for 8 years; I have worked for 4 years in 3D Architectural Design and Graphic Design.

+ My free time was filled up with modeling for my Portfolio, but also modeling some hard surface projects for Gaming Industry. Learning new programs is my way of playing games.

+ Over the years I have discovered Illustration Design and I managed to combine it with my Graphic Design skills and that is how " Music Simulation Collection " was born.

+ I also expanded my vision and designed a few UI/UX design prototypes, which led me to learn Web Design. With this experiment I created some websites. Skill that helped me build my own business and learn about marketing. By creating a business, I learned the basics of Branding, a field in which I discovered the importance of communication through simplicity and clarity.

+ Always passionate about technical things, another experiment got my attention, I disassembled old phones for fun and I thought to start a new project. It is called tablou.tech and it became a passion and a business; this is how I entered the world of Product Design.

+ This year I decided to return to the world of traditional drawing to experiment and learn more about Product Design. Drawing has been a passion since childhood, when I got in touch with drawing techniques ( Sketching, Charcoal & Pencil Techniques, Acrylic & Oil Painting ).

+ I am currently the founder of 3D Concept, a company that provides design services, where I work as a Visual Designer.

# Graphic Design

+ Being comfortable with using Photoshop, for my previous years of photo editing, I really started learning Graphic Design 8 years ago, at the same time I was learning 3D modeling.

+ Between 2020 and 2022, working from home, I had more energy and no additional project, so I learned Illustration Design. My firsts drafts were illustrated coloring books, mandalas, and logos.

# 3D Design

+ My favorite things as a child were building houses out of plasticine, playing Sims just to decorate the houses, doing puzzles, and drawing. So my 4-year job was like a dream come true. From there I was able to expand my creative abilities, trying new things. My visual representation consisted of building a portfolio of 3D projects based on personal concepts, and thus I was able to use both my technical and creative skills.

+ Working in 3D Architectural Design Industry, I became curious about Interior Design and learned some techniques to help me master my skills and discover a new mindset.

+ I was curious about each object I introduced into my scene, this made me start learning the structure of each object.

+ I have been learning, modeling, creating, building, iterating for 4 years, but my journey on the job was to respect the requirements.

+ Here are some of my projects during my time at Vego Holdings.

+ My Voxel Collection was a project I started a few years ago because I wanted to strengthen my modeling skills and discover other hidden ideas.

+ It was my favorite 3D project to work on because I had the freedom to create some stories.

# UI/UX Design

+ Planning, Organizing, Data Analysis - It was the perfect setup to develop my skills, so I gave it a try and created user interfaces, after I took a few UI/UX courses.

+ The challenge was to see if I was able to feel comfortable with the entire design process and observe my learning process to improve it.

+ Another thing I wanted to observe and strengthen was my problem-solving skills.

+ This side of Design opened up new perspectives for me. I learned the importance of user experience.

+ My first project was a case study ( Builder ) where I went through all the steps I learned.

+ Of course, I was curious to implement all my recent knowledge into a real project. This is why I started learning Web Design. It is fulfilling to be able to turn an idea into reality, into something useful.
