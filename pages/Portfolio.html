About

Visual Designer with 8+ years of experience in the Design Industry, specializing in 3D Architectural Visualization, Graphics, and Digital Products. Founder of 3D Concept, delivering 3D modeling and rendering services for Architectural and Product Visualization.

Exploration

Traditional Sketching + CAD : I reconnected with my roots in fine arts [ charcoal, pencil, acrylics, and artistic anatomy ] to bridge traditional sketching with technical design. As a personal passion, this workflow allows me to draft, refine, and model functional physical products straight from hand-drawn ideas.

Roots

Architectural Visualization : 3D Architectural Design is a major part of my background, rooted in a passion for interior design. I transform blueprints into realistic 3D spaces—focusing on precise modeling, natural lighting, textures, and spatial detail for both interiors and exteriors.

Work Experience

Architectural Experience : For 4+ years at Vego Holdings, I mastered 3D modeling and rendering while building architectural spaces and respected requirements and client briefs. To bring these projects to execution, I relied on Autodesk 3Ds Max, Autodesk AutoCAD, Chaos V-Ray, Chaos Corona Renderer, Twinmotion, Lumion, Adobe Photoshop, and Illustrator.

Exploration

Gaming : In my free time, I explored hard-surface modeling for the Gaming Industry — a space where I’ve developed conceptual thinking, patience, and precision. My previous experience in Fashion Design helped me understand the connection between 2D patterns and 3D spatial geometry. To bring these models to life, I've learned and integrated Substance Painter, Marmoset Toolbag, and KeyShot into my workflow.

Exploration

Illustration + Graphics : Exploring Illustration allowed me to blend technical drawing with Graphic Design and image manipulation. Shaped by self-learning, detailed observation, and decision-making, this long-term practice led to mastering Adobe Illustrator, developing the Music Simulation Collection, and designing digital mandalas and coloring books.

Exploration

UI/UX + Branding + Web Design : Curiosity led me to prototype UI/UX systems and learn Web Design. Building websites from scratch with Visual Studio Code laid the foundation for launching my own business, where I discovered the power of branding through simplicity, clarity, and adaptive problem-solving. To translate user needs into clean visual solutions, I rely on Figma, Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, Notion, and Typeform for UI/UX and Branding, alongside Webflow and Wix for responsive web execution.

Exploration

Product Design + Physical Art : Passionate about mechanics, I started disassembling old electronics for fun. This hands-on experiment evolved into tablou.tech — a passion-turned-business that bridged my entry into Product Design. I also explored raw textures, collecting, mapping, and converting surface finishes into tactile visual experiments.

Beyond Design

Engaging in Table Tennis and Basketball keeps my reaction time sharp and strengthens team synchronization, while Yoga and Boardgames build the spatial awareness, deep focus, and strategic vision essential to my creative process.
